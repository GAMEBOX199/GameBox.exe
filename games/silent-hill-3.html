About This Game

Silent Hill 3 follows Heather Mason, a teenager whose ordinary life is suddenlya shattered by a series of terrifying events. As she becomes trapped in the nightmare world surrounding Silent Hill, Heather must battle grotesque creatures while uncovering the disturbing truth about her past and her connection to the town.
