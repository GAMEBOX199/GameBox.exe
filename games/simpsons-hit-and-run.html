About This Game

The Simpsons: Hit & Run is an open-world action-adventure game based on the popular animated series The Simpsons. Play as Homer, Bart, Lisa, Marge, and Apu as you explore Springfield, complete missions, drive various vehicles, and uncover a mysterious conspiracy involving surveillance, strange black sedans, and a new cola drink. Featuring driving, exploration, combat, and plenty of classic Simpsons humor, Hit & Run lets you experience Springfield like never before.
