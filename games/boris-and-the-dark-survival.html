About This Game

Boris and the Dark Survival is a survival-horror game set in the mysterious world of Joey Drew Studios. You play as Boris the Wolf, who must explore the abandoned animation studio while searching for supplies and a way to escape. Explore dark and dangerous areas, collect useful items, complete objectives, and avoid the terrifying Ink Demon and other enemies lurking throughout the studio. Every run brings new challenges as you try to survive the darkness.
