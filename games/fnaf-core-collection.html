About This Game

**Five Nights at Freddy’s: Core Collection** is a collection of five classic survival-horror games from the Five Nights at Freddy’s series. Step into the role of a night-shift security guard and survive terrifying nights while monitoring cameras, managing limited resources, and avoiding deadly animatronics. The collection includes **Five Nights at Freddy’s, Five Nights at Freddy’s 2, Five Nights at Freddy’s 3, Five Nights at Freddy’s 4,** and **Five Nights at Freddy’s: Sister Location**. Experience the story and scares of the original FNAF games all in one collection.
