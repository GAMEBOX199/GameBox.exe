About This Game

Lara Croft is back, and this time the stakes are deadly. In Tomb Raider II, Lara races across the world in search of the legendary Dagger of Xian, an ancient artifact said to grant its owner the power of a dragon. From the streets of Venice to the frozen mountains of Tibet, battle ruthless enemies, uncover ancient secrets, solve challenging puzzles, and survive deadly traps. The hunt for the Dagger has begun — and Lara isn't the only one willing to kill for it.
