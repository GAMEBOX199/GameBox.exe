About This Game

Kindergarten is a dark comedy adventure game where you play as a student trapped in a bizarre and dangerous kindergarten. Explore the school, interact with strange classmates and teachers, complete different objectives, and uncover the disturbing secrets hidden beneath the seemingly normal school. Every day resets, giving you the chance to try different actions, discover new paths, and piece together what is really happening at Kindergarten.
